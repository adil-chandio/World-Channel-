## RULES

- Caption carries the story (sound-off viewers must get everything).
- Big legible type, one line at a time, 0–3 words per caption.
- Raw clip audio can run loud — it's part of the experience.
- No "Part 2". No graphics clutter. No arrows over faces.
- Last 1–1.5 s: `FULL STORY ON MONARCH CAM` card.
- Pinned comment + description line 1 = link to the long video.
