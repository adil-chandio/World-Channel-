## NARRATION (voice-02 — yehi VO banegi, ~230 words, ~2.5 min with pauses)

[HOOK] "The human opened the door… and just stood there. Frozen. I don't blame him, really. I was eating his lunch."

[BACKSTORY] "My name doesn't matter. But the humans around here call me Bandit. Torn left ear. Tiny red backpack. One rule: never go to bed hungry. It's not a bad life… but some nights, the hunger wins."

[DISCOVERY] "Three in the morning. A pickup truck in the driveway. Door unlocked. So kind. I give it a pull… and we're in. And there it was. Sandwich. Chips. Cookies. The holy trinity. I'm mid-bite when—"

[COLLISION] "The human opens the door. Frozen. He takes a picture. Really, sir? Then he comes back with a plank of wood. Buddy. This is a sandwich… not a siege."

[EXIT + HEART] "So I leave. Out the driver's side. With dignity. He wasn't angry, really. He was just… surprised. And me? I wasn't a thief. I was just hungry."

[ENDING] "If you see me some night — wave. I'll wave back. …Worth it."

## MORAL: "I wasn't a thief. I was just hungry." (empathy — show, don't tell)
## CATCHPHRASE: "…Worth it." (moon shot end)
