# NIGHT SHIFT — PSYCHOLOGY + MORAL PLAYBOOK (audience binding system)

## THE EMPATHY LOOP (har episode ka engine)
LAUGH (comedy hook) → FEEL (heart turn) → REFLECT (moral, 1 line, kabhi preachy NAHI) → SEAL ("...Worth it.")
Rule: moral DIKHAO, bolo mat. Show-don't-tell. Ek line = kaafi.

## PSYCHOLOGY TRIGGERS (use table)
1. PRATFALL EFFECT — imperfect/vulnerable character zyada loved (torn ear, bhookha bacha)
2. IDENTIFIABLE VICTIM — 1 specific character (Bandit) > general story (log ek chehre se judein)
3. PEAK-END RULE — log PEAK emotion + ENDING yaad rakhte hain = heart turn + catchphrase engineered
4. MERE EXPOSURE — repeat character + catchphrase = familiarity = pyaar ("...Worth it.")
5. CURIOSITY GAP — cold open me loop kholo ("kya hoga?") = retention
6. PARASOCIAL BOND — first-person diary POV = audience feels they KNOW Bandit
7. SOCIAL CURRENCY — share = "main caring hun" dikhana (emotional endings = share bait)
8. ZEIGARNIK (open loops) — "Bandit will return" + season arcs = subscription pressure
9. IKEA EFFECT — audience khud ideas de = ownership ("comment karo agla kya churaye!")
10. NOSTALGIA/COMFORT — chalk nights + piano + moon = comfort content = re-watch = watch-hours
