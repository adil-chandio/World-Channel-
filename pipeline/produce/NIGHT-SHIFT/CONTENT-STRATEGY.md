Cadence: 3 Bandit episodes + 1 guest per "season block".

## THE STORY SPINE (har episode — 5 beats, 40 seconds)
1. HOOK (0-3s): peak moment cold-open — himmat wala kaam shuru me hi
2. WANT (3-10s): character ka simple goal (bhookh / curiosity / fun) — relatable desire
3. COLLISION (10-25s): human world se takkar — comedy + tension (freeze, chase, plank)
4. HEART TURN (25-35s): emotional flip — "he wasn't a villain, he was just hungry/cold/lonely"
5. SIGNATURE (35-40s): catchphrase "...Worth it." + lone silhouette ending = share trigger

## EMOTIONAL TRIGGERS (rotating, har episode me 2)
vulnerability (baby/alone) · relatability (food!) · dignity (the walk) · surprise (the flip)
nostalgia (night/moon) · justice (human is clumsy, animal wins with charm) · warmth (music swell)
