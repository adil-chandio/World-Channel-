## Why this video (strategy)

- Directly adjacent to the channel's proven evergreen engine (952K bear video, Browse-led,
  55+ male TV-heavy audience).
- The concept is instantly readable and non-horror: *the same bear came back*. Curiosity, not
  shock. Accurate, not "attack" overclaiming.
- Builds the series identity the algorithm needs before we judge the reset (4 adjacent videos).

## Required clip set (detail in `pipeline/finding/CLIP_REQUESTS.md`)

1. Doorbell/Ring camera: black bear at a door/porch (hook proof)
2. The *same* bear returning — second, third angle (before/after or same-property re-shots)
3. Bear raiding cars/coolers/bins — food conditioning examples
4. A "how close" beat (backyard/pool/deck) for tension variety
5. Optional: wildlife-officer or expert context clip (bear education/food-conditioning)

## Forensic requirements

- Beat-map every clip; verify each is bear (species) and what behaviour is actually visible.
- Body-language claims (ears, posture, approach) sourced to wildlife-agency material (e.g.,
  state/provincial wildlife agency bear-behaviour guides).
- "Same bear" claims only if the source genuinely establishes it (same property/date window,
  marking/collar, or uploader statement) — otherwise frame as "a bear" / "bears like this one".
