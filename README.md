## The strategy in one paragraph

Monarch Cam becomes a **premium wildlife entertainment channel: real wildlife moments, explained
with a clear original point of view.** Growth comes from two organised series — not random uploads:

1. **Too Close: Wildlife in Human Space** (primary) — bears, roads, campsites, homes, vehicles,
   doorbell cameras, real-world close calls. TV-friendly, older US/CA-led audience, evergreen.
2. **Nature's Hidden Tricks** (secondary, clearly labelled) — camouflage, unusual animals, "can you
   spot it?" discovery. Global curiosity audience.

## Hard operating rules

1. One promise: wildlife close encounters and behaviour, told with an original Monarch Cam perspective.
2. Two organised series: `Too Close: Wildlife in Human Space` and `Nature's Hidden Tricks`.
3. No unrelated niche hopping (no karma/cars/train-crash/disaster/meme compilations).
4. **No "Part 2 / Part 3" headline titles** — playlists give continuity; titles stand alone.
5. No unverified facts; every behavioural/species claim needs a source.
6. No generic copyright disclaimer as rights clearance — every clip in `ops/rights-ledger.csv`,
   and renders refuse clips that are not `cleared`.
7. Show proof first: the opening must pay off the thumbnail within 3 seconds.
8. Build for TV and adults: clear narration, big clean visuals, natural English.
9. Every video gets a next destination: spoken end-screen handoff, related video, playlist, pinned comment.
10. Every Short connects to a long video.
11. Run at least 4 adjacent wildlife long videos before judging the reset.
