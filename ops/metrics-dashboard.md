## Diagnostic rules (blueprint §15)

| Signal | Likely interpretation | Action |
|---|---|---|
| High impressions + weak CTR | Packaging unclear / low interest | Change ONE title/thumbnail variable |
| Good CTR + sharp early retention collapse | Overpromise or slow opening | Rebuild first 30 s around proof + immediate payoff |
| Moderate launch + strong long tail | Satisfaction + evergreen fit | Next adjacent video, aggressive connection |
| Huge launch + flat line | Novelty test without durable network | Series continuity, end screens, next-video fit |
| Views but weak subscriber gain | Viewer liked a clip, not the promise | Make series identity + next-video value explicit |

## Benchmark targets (from historical winners)

- 0:30 retention ≥ **65%** (Invisible P1 did 67%, Bear original 63%)
- Avg % viewed ≥ **35%** on 7–9 min
- End-screen contribution ≥ **10%** of views (Bear P3 did 14.3%)
- Bear-audience geo: US/CA-led; watch TV share — keep TV-safe packaging

## Decision rule

Do not judge the reset before **4 adjacent long videos**. Then:
- If 2+ videos beat the retention/CTR of the old median → keep the run, extend the series.
- If all 4 flatline despite decent CTR → revisit packaging only (titles/thumbs), keep the format.
- If CTR is dead across all 4 → revisit the concept promise before anything else.
